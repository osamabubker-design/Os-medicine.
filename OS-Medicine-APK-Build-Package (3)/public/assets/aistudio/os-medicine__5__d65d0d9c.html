How it works

From prescription to pharmacy in three simple steps.

1

Upload & pay 50 ETB

Snap your prescription, drop your location, and pay the one-time search fee with telebirr or Chapa.

2

We ping nearby pharmacies

Every verified pharmacy within 5km gets your request instantly and checks their stock.

3

Get your answer fast

See who has it, chat with them, choose pickup or delivery, and rate your experience.
